Key findings
✓
Youth & corruption data is publication-grade
Youth unemployment (18.2% vs UBOS 17.9%), CPI score (26 vs TI's 25), and URA revenue figure (UGX 32.4T) are all within rounding distance of official published data.
✓
Institutional framing is well-grounded
YLP loan recovery problems, northern region HC stockouts, NMS supply chain failures, and IG discrepancy patterns all reflect documented real institutional challenges.
!
Coffee & tourism figures need updating
Coffee export revenue (seed $1.1B vs actual $2.4B) reflects FY23/24 data. Tourism revenue ($2.1B vs $1.62B) is a future projection presented as current. Both undermine PD-002 and PD-007.
!
Maternal mortality uses outdated baseline
The seed value of 264/100K live births reflects the 2019 WHO estimate. Current WHO data (2022) puts Uganda at 189/100K — a significant documented improvement the seed has not captured.
✕
EACOP completion is the critical error
Seed shows 38% pipeline completion. EACOP Company confirmed 82% by April 2026. This 44-percentage-point gap would be immediately spotted by anyone from MEMD or UNOC. Priority fix.
✕
Tax/GDP ratio and first oil timeline are off
Tax/GDP stated at 15.1% vs actual 13.4–14%. First oil target placed at "late 2027" vs the revised October 2026 commissioning date. Both are verifiable by MoFPED and MEMD stakeholders.
